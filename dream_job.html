UX Designer

I’m a UX Designer and Interaction Design student at Drexel University with a focus on creating digital experiences that are intuitive, useful, and grounded in real people’s needs. My approach combines user research, interaction design, visual design, and collaboration to turn complex problems into clear and meaningful experiences.

My Responsibilities

As a UX Designer, my responsibility is to understand a problem from the user’s perspective, identify opportunities for improvement, and translate those insights into thoughtful design solutions. I work across the design process—from research and ideation to prototyping, testing, and refinement—while collaborating with developers, researchers, product stakeholders, and other designers. I’ve applied this process across both professional and academic projects. During my experience with Tektellic, I worked on StreamLabs’ customer dashboard, redesigning humidity and temperature alert settings to make them easier to understand and prevent users from entering invalid configurations. I also designed a library search concept for the Pennsylvania Fabric Discovery Center that helped engineers more efficiently search, compare, and understand yarn-testing data. In my academic work, I’ve taken on both design and research responsibilities. For Out&Abt, a Philadelphia activities app, I conducted user interviews, contributed to the product strategy, developed the visual design system, created high- and mid-fidelity prototypes, and participated in usability and A/B testing. I currently serve as Project Manager and Secondary Researcher for APPA, where I coordinate a six-person UXID team while contributing to research and product direction.

My Qualifications

My experience has taught me how to approach design from both a **user-centered and systems-oriented perspective**. I’m comfortable conducting interviews and usability tests, synthesizing research findings, creating personas and user flows, developing wireframes and high-fidelity prototypes, and building cohesive visual systems. I also bring experience working within cross-functional teams. My projects have required me to communicate design decisions with developers and researchers, organize project timelines, manage team responsibilities, and adapt designs based on technical constraints and user feedback. As a student designer, I’ve learned that strong UX is not just about creating a visually appealing interface—it is about understanding why a problem exists and designing a solution that works for the people experiencing it. My background in visual design further shapes the way I approach UX. Through photography, illustration, branding, and interaction design, I’ve developed a strong attention to visual hierarchy, composition, and detail. At the same time, my research experience has taught me when to step back from my own design preferences and let users and evidence guide the direction. Ultimately, I see my role as connecting **people, problems, and possibilities**. Whether I’m improving an existing product, exploring a new concept, or working with a team to solve an unfamiliar problem, I want my designs to make experiences feel clearer, more accessible, and more human.
